## 📌 Business Overview
In the quick-commerce industry (10-to-20-minute delivery model), operational efficiency, inventory placement, and delivery route optimization directly drive unit economics and customer retention. 

This end-to-end data analytics project simulates and optimizes **dark store operations** and **hyper-local delivery logistics**. By leveraging spatial and order-level data, the project identifies delivery bottlenecks, models order density across zones, and delivers actionable business insights via interactive reporting.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Impact
- **Dark Store SLA Compliance:** Evaluated order processing times to identify dark store operational bottlenecks.
- **Delivery Time Analysis:** Segmented delivery durations across distance metrics, traffic conditions, and time-of-day peaks.
- **Inventory & Order Density Mapping:** Modeled high-volume delivery zones to optimize rider distribution and dark store inventory allocation.
- **Actionable Business Recommendations:** Formulated strategy blueprints to decrease average delivery turn-around time (TAT).
